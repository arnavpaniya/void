# VOID POC plan

## Direction

Build a colourful, playful graphic-tee storefront using temporary brand and product assets. Keep shopping readable and fast, with one contained immersive feature rather than continuous 3D across every section.

## Reference takeaways

- Infinite Passerella: a distinct fashion world and a sense of spectacle around the garment.
- Chrono: a 3D-led introduction that transitions into garment discovery and product detail.
- VOID direction: Gen Z graphic culture, saturated print colour, oversized typography, and interactions that work by touch as well as pointer.

## POC experience

1. Bold landing area and a featured tee.
2. An interactive carousel / runway moment for the drop.
3. **Ink-burst preloader** as the signature opening: oversized VOID lettering introduces a flat graphic tee, and a vivid ink shape reveals its temporary print before blooming into the storefront. Keep it 2D, quick and touch-friendly; do not use the Anatomy-made factory illustration in this component.
4. Lightweight product collection and sample cart flow.
5. Static visual fallback and touch-first controls for the interactive scene.

## Preloader motion and performance

- Reveal temporary artwork on the tee with an organic ink shape, then expand that shape into the transition to the page.
- Keep the background a single saturated colour, use the supplied wordmark reference for display typography only, and keep copy to a minimum.
- Use 2D SVG/CSS rather than WebGL, include a skip control, respect reduced motion, and size the tee composition for phones.
- Current tee graphic and wording are placeholders. Replace them with client-approved artwork and type when available.

## E-commerce boundary

The POC uses sample product data and a front-end cart. If the client approves the concept, confirm Shopify catalog, checkout, inventory, and fulfillment requirements before choosing the production integration.

## Placeholder policy

All current identity, copy, garment model, textures, garment graphics, prices, and products are provisional. Replace them with client-approved materials after direction approval.
